04 / One last thing

A letter for
Piggy.

My Piggy,

Five years. I genuinely don’t know how I’ve lasted this long with you… or, to be fair, how you’ve lasted this long with me. From our first date at Brunel where we barely spoke and I was far too high to be useful, to buying our first home together — it’s mad looking back at how much life we’ve already done side by side.

You’ve brought so much energy into my life. Your random silliness, your stupid jokes, that smile, the way you take care of me — once you’ve had enough “kululu”, obviously — and all the little things you do that constantly remind me what proper wifey material looks like.

I love that we can take the piss out of each other one minute and be there for each other completely the next. I love our food obsession, our trips, the memories with both our families, and even the normal boring days where it’s just us being us. You somehow make all of it mean something.

Buying our house together this year made me realise just how far we’ve come. It isn’t just a house to me. It’s something we built towards together, a place for our next chapter, and probably the biggest reminder yet that I don’t just love the life we’ve had — I’m excited for the life we’re still building.

I joke about not knowing how I’ve survived five years with you, Piggy, but the truth is I wouldn’t trade those five years for anything. I’m proud of us, I’m grateful for you, and after everything we’ve been through, I’d still choose you. Again and again.

Here’s to more food, more travelling, more stupid jokes, more chaos, more “kululu”, and a lot more years of you calling me a dumbass.

Forever yours, ♥
